🧠 NeuroSync

Gamified Stroke Rehabilitation Platform

NeuroSync is a computer-vision-based stroke rehabilitation platform that transforms therapeutic hand and arm movements into interactive games.

The system uses a camera and hand-tracking technology to detect patient movements, provide real-time feedback, and record rehabilitation performance.


🎯 Problem

Stroke rehabilitation often involves repetitive exercises that can become difficult to follow consistently.

Patients may experience:

- Reduced hand mobility
- Poor coordination
- Difficulty with repetitive exercises
- Fatigue during rehabilitation
- Lack of continuous progress tracking

NeuroSync addresses these challenges through an interactive and gamified rehabilitation experience.


💡 Solution

NeuroSync combines:

- 🖐️ Hand tracking
- 🎮 Rehabilitation games
- 📊 Performance tracking
- 🤖 Adaptive difficulty
- 🔐 Patient privacy
- 👤 Individual patient profiles

The patient interacts with games using their hand movements captured through a camera.


✨ Features

🎮 Multiple Rehabilitation Levels

NeuroSync provides multiple game-based rehabilitation levels with progressively challenging movements.

The games focus on:

- Hand movement
- Reach and coordination
- Reaction
- Accuracy
- Motor control

🖐️ Real-Time Hand Tracking

Camera input is processed using MediaPipe Hands to identify hand landmarks and track movement.

📈 Performance Tracking

The system records useful session information such as:

- Score
- Accuracy
- Reaction time
- Completed targets
- Session duration
- Level progress

🤖 Adaptive Difficulty

The game can adjust its difficulty according to the patient's performance.

Higher performance
        ↓
Higher challenge

Lower performance
        ↓
Reduced challenge

😴 Fatigue Indicators

Changes in speed, accuracy, reaction time, and missed interactions can be monitored to identify possible signs of fatigue.

🔐 Patient Privacy

NeuroSync provides:

- Secure login
- Individual patient profiles
- Patient-specific session records
- Controlled access to information
- Privacy-focused data handling

📊 Progress Dashboard

Patients can view their rehabilitation history and performance across different sessions.


🌟 Novelty

NeuroSync goes beyond simple hand tracking by combining computer vision with rehabilitation gamification.

Key innovations

1. Rehabilitation-as-a-Game
Therapeutic movements are converted into interactive gameplay.

2. Adaptive Gameplay
Difficulty can change according to patient performance.

3. Performance-Based Fatigue Awareness
Gameplay performance can provide indicators of possible fatigue.

4. Long-Term Progress Tracking
Patient sessions can be stored and compared over time.

5. Privacy-Focused Patient Records
Patient information and rehabilitation data are maintained separately for individual users.

6. Hardware-Light Approach
A standard camera can be used instead of specialized rehabilitation hardware.


🔐 Privacy

NeuroSync follows a privacy-focused approach.

- Patient accounts are individually authenticated.
- Patient records are separated by user.
- Rehabilitation sessions are stored for progress tracking.
- Camera input is used for real-time interaction.
- Raw video is not unnecessarily stored.
- Patient information is not exposed to other users.


🚀 Future Scope

Future versions can include:

- Advanced tremor analysis

- Therapist dashboard

- More rehabilitation games

⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

NeuroSync is a technology prototype for demonstrating computer-vision-assisted rehabilitation. It is not a replacement for professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or supervised rehabilitation.
